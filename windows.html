Before you open it

Windows downloads are unsigned, so Windows may show a publisher warning. Download only from this site or the linked GitHub releases.

New installations save notes in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Jotilla\notes. Updates keep your existing archive and startup choice. You can move or switch archives from the app. Uninstall preserves your notes.

Back up the whole archive, including notes.ini and RTF files.
